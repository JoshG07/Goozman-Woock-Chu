Selects all verifiable observations of plant invasive species in California


Spatial and Temporal Coverage:
The Prescribed Fire dataset includes prescribed fires across all of California from 1900 to 2025
The Invasive Plants Species dataset includes iNaturalist species observations data across all of California from 2009 to 2026.

Permissions/Restrictions:
No permissions required to access the two public data sets.
